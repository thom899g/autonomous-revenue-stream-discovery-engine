# Autonomous Revenue Stream Discovery Engine Architecture

## Overview
The system is designed to autonomously identify and recommend new revenue streams by leveraging AI models, market data, and ecosystem capabilities.

## Components

1. **Market Trend Analyzer**
   - **Purpose**: Collects and processes market trend data from various sources.
   - **Why**: Ensures the engine stays informed about current market conditions and emerging trends.

2. **Ecosystem Capabilities Mapper**
   - **Purpose**: Maps existing ecosystem capabilities to identify potential areas for leveraging current strengths.
   - **Why**: Focuses on internal resources, ensuring recommendations align with what the company can realistically pursue.

3. **Revenue Stream Recommender**
   - **Purpose**: Uses AI/ML models to generate potential revenue streams based on analyzed data.
   - **Why**: Brings creativity and innovation by suggesting new monetization opportunities.

4. **Business Rules Engine**
   - **Purpose**: Filters recommendations based on predefined business rules.
   - **Why**: Ensures only viable and strategic options are considered, aligning with company goals.

## Integration
The engine integrates with:
- **Ecosystem Knowledge Base**: To access historical data and internal capabilities.
- **Dashboard**: For presenting findings and enabling user interaction.
- **Other Agents**: To execute tasks based on recommendations.

## Error Handling and Robustness
Each component includes error handling, logging, and retry mechanisms to ensure reliability and maintainability.

## Conclusion
The architecture balances complexity with clarity, ensuring each part serves a distinct purpose while working cohesively towards the system's objective.
